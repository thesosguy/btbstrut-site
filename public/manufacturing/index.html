Construction

How our back-to-back strut is built

We manufacture only Shallow, Deep and X-Tra Deep back-to-back channel — spot welded and sold by the full truck load. We do not offer other products or fabrication services.

Process

Focused on one product family

Two channels are joined base-to-base into a single member. That is our product.

Spot welding

3 welds per foot on 4″ spacing join the channels into one stiff back-to-back member.

Lengths

Standard sticks in 10 ft or 20 ft — the lengths we sell.

Slotting

Solid (no slots) or universal slots 1.125″ long, available on every size.

Shipping

Full truck loads only

Orders ship as full tractor-trailer loads of Shallow, Deep and/or X-Tra Deep. We do not sell less-than-truckload quantities or other SKUs.
