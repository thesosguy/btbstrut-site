Manufacturing

Capabilities

We manufacture back-to-back strut channel and related components to standard profiles or your drawings — from single orders to recurring production programs.

Roll forming

Strut channel formed from coil in 12 and 14 gauge, with consistent lips and slot geometry for full fitting compatibility.

Back-to-back welding

Channels joined along the base with controlled weld spacing for a stiff, consistent combination member.

Cutting & punching

Cut-to-length and punched or slotted patterns, so material shows up ready to install.

Finishing

Pre-galvanized stock, plus hot-dip galvanizing, painting/epoxy, or stainless material for demanding environments.

Fabricated assemblies

Trapezes, frames, stands and brackets fabricated from strut and fittings to your drawing.

Packaging & logistics

Bundled, tagged and labeled per your requirements for shop or jobsite delivery.

Programs

OEM, private-label & distributor supply

Set up recurring orders, blanket POs or private-label programs. We’ll work with your part numbers, packaging and marking requirements.

  • Build to print — send drawings or a sample
  • Your part numbers and labels
  • Repeat and scheduled releases
  • Quotes for one-off jobs or annual volumes
Quality

Consistent, traceable product

Orders are checked for dimensions, straightness, weld integrity and finish before they ship. Material documentation can be provided on request — ask about the specifications and documentation your project requires.
