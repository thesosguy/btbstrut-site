Back-to-back strut channel

Welded double channel in standard profiles, gauges and finishes — plus custom lengths, punching and fittings. Every order is made to your spec; request a quote for pricing and lead time.

Profiles

Channel profiles

All profiles are 1-5/8″ wide and compatible with standard 1-5/8″ strut fittings, channel nuts and hardware.

Standard back-to-back

1-5/8″ × 3-1/4″ — two 1-5/8″ deep channels welded together. The workhorse for trapezes, racks and frames.

12 ga14 gaB22A / P1001 style

Shallow back-to-back

1-5/8″ × 1-5/8″ — two 13/16″ shallow channels welded together, for tight spaces and lighter loads.

Ask for gauges

Deep back-to-back

Built from deeper channel (e.g. 2-7/16″ or 3-1/4″) for long spans and heavy equipment supports.

Custom / on request

Typical specifications

AttributeStandard options
Width1-5/8″ (41.3 mm)
Combined height3-1/4″ standard; 1-5/8″ shallow; deeper profiles on request
Material thickness12 gauge (approx. 0.105″ / 2.6 mm) or 14 gauge (approx. 0.075″ / 1.9 mm)
ConstructionTwo channels joined by intermittent or continuous welds along the base
Lengths10′ and 20′ standard; cut-to-length available
Hole patternsSolid, slotted, punched holes (e.g. 9/16″ on centers), or custom patterns
MaterialsCarbon steel, stainless steel (Type 304 / 316), aluminum on request

Dimensions shown are the common industry profile for 1-5/8″ strut systems. Exact tolerances, section properties and load data are provided with your quote — always confirm load capacity with published engineering data and a qualified engineer for your application.
