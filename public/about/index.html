About BTB Strut Systems

A manufacturer focused on one thing: back-to-back strut channel and the components that go with it.

Focused on double strut

BTB Strut Systems manufactures back-to-back welded strut channel for the electrical, mechanical, solar and industrial markets. By concentrating on this product, we can offer the profiles, gauges, finishes and custom options our customers need — with the responsiveness of working directly with the manufacturer.

Whether you’re a contractor who needs material on site, an OEM building equipment frames, or a distributor stocking strut, we’re ready to quote your next order.
