Construction

Spot welded for the long haul

Each member is two channels joined base-to-base with 3 spot welds per foot (welds on 4″ spacing). Choose solid (no slots) or universal slots — 1.125″ long — on every size.

  • Shallow, Deep or X-Tra Deep only
  • 10 ft or 20 ft sticks
  • Solid or universal slots
  • Full tractor-trailer loads only

Why back-to-back

One stiff member. Two faces to attach.

Back-to-back strut gives you a high-capacity framing member with open faces on both sides — sized for the same hardware conventions as single strut.

Two-sided attachment

Open faces on both sides of the welded member.

Three depth options

Shallow, Deep and X-Tra Deep cover common load and clearance needs.

Truckload supply

Ordered and shipped as full tractor-trailer loads.

Applications

Where double strut carries the system

Back-to-back channel is used wherever single strut can’t span far enough or carry enough — supporting electrical, mechanical, solar and industrial framing.

Electrical

Trapeze hangers, cable tray and panel supports.

Mechanical

Pipe racks, HVAC supports and multi-tier runs.

Solar

Racking members and equipment stands.

Industrial

Frames, stands and process supports.
