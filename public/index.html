Manufacturer · Back-to-back strut channel

Double strut, built to carry the load.

BTB Strut Systems manufactures back-to-back welded strut channel — two 1-5/8″ channels joined into one stiff, high-capacity member — plus the fittings and hardware to complete the system. Standard and custom lengths, gauges and finishes for contractors, OEMs and distributors.

Why back-to-back

More stiffness. Longer spans. Fewer supports.

Welding two strut channels back to back creates a box-like section with a much higher moment of inertia than a single channel — so it spans farther and carries heavier loads, while keeping a continuous slot on both faces for attachments.

Two-sided slot

Open slots on both faces let you attach clamps, nuts and fittings from either side of the member.

Higher capacity

The combined section resists bending far better than single channel — ideal for trapeze hangers, racks and frames.

Cut to your length

Standard 10′ and 20′ sticks, or cut-to-length pieces ready to install.

Finish for the environment

Pre-galvanized, hot-dip galvanized, painted, stainless or plain — matched to indoor, outdoor or corrosive jobs.

We make it
